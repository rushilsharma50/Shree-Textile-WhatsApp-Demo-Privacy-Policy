Privacy Policy

Shree Textile WhatsApp Demo

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. About This Demo

Shree Textile WhatsApp Demo is a demonstration project designed to test automated responses to WhatsApp messages and provide sample textile-related information. It is not necessarily an official customer-service channel for a registered business.

2. Information We Process

When you message this demo, it may process your WhatsApp phone number, message content, message timestamps, and technical message metadata needed to receive, process, and respond to your message.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate, test, troubleshoot, and improve the demonstration workflow and to generate relevant automated replies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Service Providers and Processing

Messages may be processed through Meta's WhatsApp services and the n8n automation workflow used to operate this demo. The workflow may also use infrastructure or networking services required to make the demo accessible. Those providers may process technical information as part of delivering their services.

5. Data Retention

Message information may remain in automation execution records or technical logs for testing and troubleshooting. Retention depends on the configuration of the services used. Records may be deleted when no longer needed for the demo, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information used by this demo. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information to this demo.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can stop interacting with the demo at any time. For questions about this policy or to request access to or deletion of information associated with your messages, contact the project operator using the email address below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

This demonstration is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit children's personal information.

This policy may be updated if the demo or its data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
